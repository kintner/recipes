# Creamy Olive Oil Tomato Soup

Half batch of J. Kenji López-Alt's 15-Minute Creamy (Vegan) Tomato Soup. Olive oil and bread make it creamy without dairy.

- **Yield:** About 2 servings
- **Time:** About 15 minutes

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :---- | ----: |
| Extra-virgin olive oil, divided | 1/4 cup / 60 ml |
| Garlic, grated on a microplane | 1 clove |
| Small onion, finely sliced | 1/2 (about 1/2 cup) |
| Dried oregano | 1/2 teaspoon |
| Red pepper flakes | 1/4 teaspoon |
| White bread, crusts removed, torn into 1/2-inch pieces | 1 slice |
| Whole peeled tomatoes packed in juice | 1 (28-ounce) can |
| Water | 1 cup |
| Kosher salt and black pepper | To taste |
| Minced chives, basil, or parsley | Optional, for garnish |

## Method

1. **Soften the aromatics.** Heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the garlic, onion, oregano, and pepper flakes. Cook, stirring often, until the onion softens without browning, about 4 minutes.
2. **Add bread and tomatoes.** Stir in the bread and the tomatoes with their juice. Break up the tomatoes with a potato masher, whisk, or your hands.
3. **Simmer.** Add the water, bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce the heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. **Blend.** Carefully transfer the soup to a blender, venting the lid and covering it with a towel. With the blender running on high, slowly drizzle in the remaining 3 tablespoons of olive oil and blend until completely smooth. An immersion blender works too, though the soup will be slightly less silky.
5. **Season and serve.** Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with herbs and a drizzle of olive oil if you like. Serve with bread or grilled cheese.
