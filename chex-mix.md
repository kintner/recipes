# Chex Mix

- **Oven:** 250 F
- **Bake:** 1 hour

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :---- | ----: |
| Corn Chex cereal | 1 cup |
| Rice Chex cereal | 1 cup |
| Wheat Chex cereal | 2 cups |
| Mixed nuts | 1/2 cup |
| Bite-size pretzels | 1 1/2 cups |
| Butter | 3 tablespoons |
| Worcestershire sauce | 2 1/2 tablespoons |
| Seasoned salt | 3/4 teaspoon |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 teaspoon |
| Onion powder | 1/2 teaspoon |
| Cayenne | 1/4 teaspoon |

## Method

1. **Preheat.** Heat the oven to 250 F.
2. **Make the coating.** Melt the butter, then stir in the Worcestershire sauce, seasoned salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and cayenne.
3. **Combine.** In a large bowl, mix the cereals, nuts, and pretzels. Pour the butter mixture over and stir until evenly coated.
4. **Bake.** Spread in an ungreased roasting pan or on a rimmed sheet pan. Bake for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes.
5. **Cool.** Spread on paper towels to cool. Store in an airtight container.
