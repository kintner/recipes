# Pizza Sauce

## Detroit-Style Pizza Sauce

From J. Kenji López-Alt's Detroit-style pizza recipe (Serious Eats).

- **Yield:** About 3 cups

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :---- | ----: |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 2 tablespoons / 30 ml |
| Garlic, minced | 3 medium cloves |
| Dried oregano | 2 teaspoons / about 5 g |
| Red pepper flakes | Dash |
| High-quality crushed tomatoes | 1 (28-ounce / 800 g) can |
| Granulated garlic powder | 1 teaspoon / about 6 g |
| Granulated onion powder | 1 teaspoon / about 6 g |
| Sugar | 1 tablespoon / about 15 g |
| Kosher salt | To taste |

### Method

1. **Bloom the aromatics.** In a saucepan over medium heat, cook the olive oil, garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
2. **Add the tomatoes.** Stir in the crushed tomatoes, garlic powder, onion powder, and sugar.
3. **Reduce.** Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until reduced to about 3 cups, about 30 minutes. Start checking at 25 minutes.
4. **Season.** Add salt to taste.
