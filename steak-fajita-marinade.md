# Steak Fajita Marinade

From J. Kenji López-Alt's [Grilled Skirt Steak Fajitas](https://www.seriouseats.com/grilled-skirt-steak-fajitas-food-lab-recipe) (Serious Eats).

- **Marinate:** 3-10 hours, refrigerated

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :---- | ----: |
| Soy sauce | 1/2 cup / 120 ml |
| Lime juice (6-8 limes) | 1/2 cup / 120 ml |
| Canola oil | 1/2 cup / 120 ml |
| Brown sugar, packed | 1/4 cup / 55 g |
| Chili powder (see notes) | 1 tablespoon |
| Garlic, finely minced | 3 medium cloves / about 1 tablespoon |
| Ground cumin seed | 2 teaspoons |
| Freshly ground black pepper | 2 teaspoons |
| Trimmed skirt steak (see notes), cut crosswise into 5- to 6-inch pieces | 2 lb / 900 g |

## Method

1. **Mix.** Whisk together everything except the steak. Set aside 1/2 cup of the marinade to toss with sliced peppers and onions for the fajitas.
2. **Marinate.** Put the steak in a gallon zipper-lock bag with the rest of the marinade. Squeeze out the air, seal, and massage until the meat is coated.
3. **Refrigerate.** Lay the bag flat in the fridge for 3-10 hours, turning every couple of hours.
4. **Before cooking.** Remove the steak and wipe off excess marinade.

## Notes

- **Steak substitutes:** Hanger, sirloin flap (sold as sirloin tip in New England, not the same as sirloin steak), or flank steak.
- **Chili powder:** For the best flavor, grind your own from equal parts ancho and guajillo chiles.
