# Brown Butter Fruitful Crispy Treats

Variant of NYT Cooking's [Caramelized Brown Butter Rice Krispies Treats](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/11587-caramelized-brown-butter-rice-krispies-treats), with a quarter to a third of the cereal swapped for Cascadian Farm Mini Fruitfuls.

- **Yield:** About 30 bars (half sheet pan)
- **Time:** 20 minutes, plus cooling

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :---- | ----: |
| Unsalted butter | 8 ounces |
| Mini marshmallows | 20 ounces |
| Kosher salt | 1/4 teaspoon |
| Rice Krispies | 9 ounces (or 8 for fruitier) |
| Mini Fruitfuls | 3 ounces (or 4 for fruitier) |
| Flaky salt | Optional |

## Method

1. **Prep.** Line a rimmed half sheet pan with parchment.
2. **Brown the butter.** Melt the butter in a large pot over medium-low heat. Stir until it's brown and smells nutty.
3. **Brown the marshmallows.** Add the marshmallows and kosher salt. Stir until melted, then until light caramel colored, about 5 minutes.
4. **Mix.** Turn off the heat. Stir in the Rice Krispies, then gently fold in the Mini Fruitfuls.
5. **Press and cool.** Press lightly into the pan and top with flaky salt. Cool, then cut.

## Notes

- **Ratio:** 9:3 is balanced, with brown butter leading. 8:4 is fruitier and crunchier.
- Measure the cereal by weight. Mini Fruitfuls are denser than Rice Krispies.
- Half batch: use a 9-by-13-inch pan.
