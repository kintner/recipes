# Pizza Dough

## No-Knead Pan Pizza Dough

Makes enough for one small pan pizza.

- **Pan:** 10-inch carbon steel skillet
- **Oven:** 500 F

| Ingredient | Amount | Baker's % |
| :---- | ----: | ----: |
| Bread flour | 160 g | 100% |
| Water | 110 g | 69% |
| Salt | 4 g | 2.5% |
| Yeast | 1.6 g | 1% |
| Olive oil | 3.2 g | 2% |

### Method

1. **Mix.** Combine all ingredients until no dry flour remains.
2. **Bulk rest.** Cover and rest at room temperature for 8-24 hours.
3. **Shape.** Turn out the dough and shape it into a ball. Place it in a pan coated with olive oil.
4. **Proof.** Cover and let rise for 2 hours.
5. **Stretch.** Flip the dough so it's coated in oil, then stretch it to fill the pan. Make sure there are no bubbles or air pockets underneath.
6. **Bake.** Top and bake at 500 F.

### 9 x 13 Cast Iron Casserole Variant

Same dough and method, scaled 2x.

- **Pan:** 9 x 13-inch cast iron casserole pan
- **Oven:** 500 F

| Ingredient | Amount | Baker's % |
| :---- | ----: | ----: |
| Bread flour | 320 g | 100% |
| Water | 220 g | 69% |
| Salt | 8 g | 2.5% |
| Yeast | 3.2 g | 1% |
| Olive oil | 6.4 g | 2% |

**Sizing:** The casserole floor has about 2.35x the area of the skillet bottom,
so 2x gives a slightly thinner crust than the skillet version.

**Differences from the skillet version:**

- Use more oil in the pan to coat the larger floor and corners.
- If the dough springs back from the corners while stretching, cover it, rest
  10-15 minutes, then stretch again.
- Keep the oven at 500 F; going hotter burns the toppings before the cast iron
  catches up. Bake on the lowest rack for a longer time, and check that the
  underside is golden before pulling it. If the top is done but the bottom is
  pale, give it a few more minutes on the lowest rack.
